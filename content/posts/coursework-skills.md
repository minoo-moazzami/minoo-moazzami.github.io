## Relevant Coursework

##### in reverse chronological order:		


**Logic Programming**  
A master’s course taught by [Dr. Majid Alizadeh](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=cgvRmcwAAAAJ&hl=en), covering the following topics: Untyped Lambda Calculus, Basic Type Theory, Simply Typed Lambda Calculus, The Curry-Howard Correspondence, Polymorphism in Type Theory and programming in Rocq proof assistant. In the programming part we studied through the first volume of [Software Foundations](https://softwarefoundations.cis.upenn.edu/lf-current/index.html).


**Logic for Computer Science**  
An introductory master’s course taught by [Dr. Mohamad Ardeshir](http://math.sharif.ir/faculties/mardeshir) covering topics in non‑classical logic, with main focus on Modal and Intuitionistic logic. In this course we learned the philosophy and theory of non-classical logics and how they are used in the formal foundation of computer science.


**Theory of Language and Automata**   
A standard Automata & Theory of Computation course covering topics from simple Automata up to Turing Machine. Our main source for this course was. Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages and Computation, by Hopcroft, Motwani & Ullman.


**Logic & Foundation of Mathematics (ZFC set theory)**  
Both courses taught by [Dr. Mohamad Ardeshir](http://math.sharif.ir/faculties/mardeshir) to whom I owe most of my knowledge in this field. These courses were my formal introduction to mathematical logic and where I first discovered my passion for it. I passed both courses with full mark and was a teaching assistant for them several times in the following semesters, holding weekly supplemental and problem solving classes and grading papers.
